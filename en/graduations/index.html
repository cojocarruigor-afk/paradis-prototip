Frequently asked questions

How do we split the cost?

Simply: the price is fixed per person, so everyone's share is known from the start.

Can we stay late?

Yes. We don't charge for overtime — it's written into the contract.

Can the price change after signing the contract?

No. The final price is menu × number of guests and it is guaranteed in the contract: no hall fee, no service charge, no overtime fees, no corkage. It changes only if you change the guest count or the menu.

Can we bring our own drinks?

Yes, at no charge. Or, if you prefer, we'll supply the drinks at special prices — whichever suits you.

Is there parking?

Yes, our own parking for your guests, right at the hall.
