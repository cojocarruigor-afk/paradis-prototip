Grand Hall

The Grand Hall seats up to 80 guests at round tables or one long imperial table. It has a high ceiling, natural light, light curtains and gold candelabra on the tables.

There is room for dancing, a live band or a DJ, and a separate area for a candy bar or welcome drink.

  • Up to 80 seated guests
  • Round tables or imperial table
  • Dance floor and space for live music
  • Gold candelabra and floral arrangements
  • Area for candy bar and welcome drink

Smarald Hall

The Smarald Hall is designed for small events of up to 25 guests: one long table, emerald accents, fresh flowers and gold-rimmed plates.

It is the right choice for a small christening, a family birthday, a business dinner or a department party.

  • Up to 25 guests
  • Elegant imperial table
  • Full privacy for your group
  • Suits business dinners
  • Floral décor included
