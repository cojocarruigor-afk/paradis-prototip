About Paradis

A banquet hall run by someone who has been hosting celebrations since 1990.

Constantin Șeremet

Director, Paradis Banquet Hall

Constantin Șeremet has worked in hospitality since 1990. Over more than three decades he has opened and built up more than 20 banquet halls and restaurants.

From each one he kept what works: a kitchen that cooks for your table, a floor team that knows the rhythm of a celebration, and a hall that stays yours all evening.

Paradis is where all of that experience comes together: a large white-and-gold hall full of light, and a smaller one for family tables.
